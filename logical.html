Rules the database enforces

  • The scene source must match the year: 2018–2024 use the original scenes, 2025–2026 the alternative scenes.
  • A period always ends after it starts, and both years lie in 2018–2026.
  • Uncertainty is above zero, coverage is between 0 and 100, probability and confidence are between 0 and 1.
  • One rate per sector, period and method, so another method can be stored beside the first.
  • Deleting an erosion rate deletes its assessment.

Third normal form: every attribute depends on the key of its own entity and nothing else. Risk limits and Confidence weights live in their own entities, so changing a rule means changing a row, not the design.
