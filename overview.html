Shoreline Analytics

Provisional results

A PostGIS database for the satellite shoreline of Alexandria. It holds nine years of Sentinel-2 shorelines, the rate at which the shoreline moves along 145 transects, a Risk Class for each and a Confidence Score that says how far to trust it.

What the data says

  • In every sector the median transect moves slightly seaward (+0.2 to +0.9 m a year), so no sector shows clear erosion overall.
  • Four transects in Montaza Bay show accretion that holds up statistically. They are local, not a trend of the whole bay.
  • 13 transects fall in the High or Very high class, but none has a high Confidence Score. Treat them as places to check, not as findings.
  • Risk describes how big the change is. Confidence describes how strong the evidence is. They are kept apart on purpose.

Limits to keep in mind

  • All shorelines and rates are provisional. Independent validation is still pending.
  • The shoreline is a water-mask proxy at 10 m resolution, not a surveyed line.
  • Landsat 9 was compared for 2026 only, so it does not validate the other years.
  • Most transects fit a straight line poorly (median R squared 0.13). Only 4 of 145 pass the corrected significance test.
  • The Confidence Score weights are this project's proposal. They are stored in a table and can be changed.
